Why Stuff for Sailors

Made by someone who actually lives this life.

A classically-trained chef, former commercial fisherman and lobsterman, and lifelong sailor who lives aboard a 40-foot sailboat in Seattle — bringing decades of real hands-on marine and small-space experience to every design.

Built to Fit, Not Force

Every boat, van, and RV is a little different. Rather than adapt your space to a generic part, we design the part around your exact measurements, your exact gear, and your exact mounting spot.

Marine-Durable Materials

ASA and carbon-fiber-reinforced filaments for UV and weather exposure, PETG for interior use. Chosen for the marine environment, not just for looking good on a shelf.

Local, Personal, Fast

Design, 3D printing, and installation — all handled directly, with a prototype-first process so you see the fit before we commit to the final build.

On the Horizon — Remote & Offshore Support

A solid fix, to get you home after "it" breaks.

Find yourself in a remote anchorage after a tough passage with a broken sheave, fairlead, clam cleat, stanchion base, or the like? Send a photo or the broken part itself, and I can scan it or design an acceptable placeholder — printed and ready to get you back in action.

Not the end-all part. The right-now part — a solid stand-in that holds until you can get to a proper chandlery.

  • Sheaves & blocks
  • Fairleads
  • Clam cleats
  • Stanchion bases
  • Hatch & latch hardware
  • Odd brackets & fittings

How It Works

Scan, sketch, or send the broken piece.

A photo with something for scale (a coin, a ruler, your thumb) is often enough to start. If you have the broken part, even better.

Design & Print

A placeholder built to hold.

Not a permanent replacement — a genuinely solid stand-in, built the same honest way as everything else here: made for exactly what you need right now.

Back In Action

You get home. Then get the real part.

Once you're back to a proper chandlery or rigger, swap in the permanent fix on your own schedule — no longer stuck waiting on it offshore.
