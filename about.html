About Stuff for Sailors

A life on the water, put to work.

Stuff for Sailors is a one-person shop — design, printing, and installation, all done by someone who actually lives the mobile lifestyle he builds for.

A classically-trained chef, a former commercial fisherman and lobsterman, and a lifelong sailor who lives aboard a 40-foot sailboat in Seattle.

That combination isn't a marketing angle — it's just what the founder's career actually looks like. Decades in professional kitchens taught precision and a respect for tools that do their one job well. Years as a commercial fisherman and lobsterman on the East Coast meant living with gear that either worked in a real seaway or got replaced fast. And full-time life aboard a sailboat means every storage problem, every awkward mounting spot, every "why doesn't anyone make a part for this exact space" moment is one he's run into himself, on his own boat, before ever building it for someone else.

3D printing changed what was possible with that experience. Instead of a one-off carpentry job or a bin that almost fits, a part can now be designed to the exact millimeter of a specific locker, a specific radio, a specific pair of binoculars — then printed in a material built to survive sun, salt, and motion.

Stuff for SailorsTurning that toolkit into custom gear for anyone who makes a home in a small, moving space — on the water or on the road.

Why "Because Sailors Love Their Stuff"

Anyone who's lived aboard, driven a van build-out, or outfitted an RV knows the feeling: you love your gear, you just wish it fit better. That's the whole business, in one sentence — practical, well-made pieces for the stuff you already own and already care about.

Every piece is designed and made to order. No inventory, no guessing sizes — a prototype confirms the fit before the final build ever gets made.
